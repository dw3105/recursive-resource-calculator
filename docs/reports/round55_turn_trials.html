Turn trials built, measured, and shipped off: no blueprint changes

Explain very simply: drawn pack turns each Block by a rule and never checks another Turn. Round 55 built a step that tries other Turns one Block at a time and keeps a try only if the blueprint gets cheaper. Measured at the game's tick rate, a try costs about as much as the whole search before it, the rule's Turn is already cheapest or tied on most sheets, and the few wins are 2–6% less area at about 6× the generation time. The player's order is valid, then fast, then frugal, so the step ships off (player Q11 a). Every player blueprint is byte-identical to round 54.
